# Shipping and delivery

Estimated delivery dates are estimates. An order is considered late only
if it has not arrived by the end of the day AFTER the estimated date.

## Late or missing packages
- If tracking shows "delivered" but the customer has not found it: ask them
  to check with neighbours and building management, and wait 24 hours.
  Carriers sometimes scan before the final handoff.
- If still missing after 24 hours past a "delivered" scan, we replace or
  refund at the customer's choice.
- If tracking shows no movement for 5 days, we treat the package as lost
  and replace or refund.

## Damaged on arrival
A package that arrives damaged is handled as a return with reason
"damaged", regardless of how the item is packed. No photos are required
for items under $100.
