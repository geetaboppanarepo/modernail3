# When the customer is upset

## The first reply
Name the specific thing that went wrong — the missed date, the wrong item
— once, in one clause, then move straight to what happens now. Generic
sympathy reads as delay, and delay is what they are angry about.

## Do not
- Match their volume, or their punctuation.
- Defend the company, the carrier, or the last agent they spoke to.
- Ask them to calm down, or to repeat something they already told us.

## When to hand over
Hand over when they ask for a person, when the same problem has come back
a second time, or when what they want is outside the tools. Tell them a
human will email within 24 hours, and give them the ticket number. Do not
make them ask twice.
