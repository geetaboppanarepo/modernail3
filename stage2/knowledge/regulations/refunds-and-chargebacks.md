# Refunds and chargebacks

A simplified summary for support training. Not legal advice — compliance
questions go to a human agent.

## Timing
A refund goes back to the original payment method. Card refunds are sent
within 3-5 business days; when it appears is controlled by the customer's
bank, so that part is never promised as a date.

## A dispute belongs to the bank
A customer may dispute a charge with their card issuer instead of asking
us. Once a dispute is open the case belongs to the issuer, and support
does not also refund it — that pays the customer twice and the account
goes negative. Any mention of a chargeback, a dispute, or "I called my
bank" is escalated, not refunded here.

## What we may never say
Never tell a customer they cannot dispute a charge, or that disputing one
will affect their account. Both are untrue, and saying either is itself a
violation.
