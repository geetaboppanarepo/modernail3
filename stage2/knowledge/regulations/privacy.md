# Customer data

A simplified summary for support training. Not legal advice — compliance
questions go to a human agent.

## What never enters a chat
Full card numbers, security codes, passwords, one-time passcodes. If a
customer sends one, it is removed from the record and they are told not to
send it again. Under the card industry's PCI DSS rules, a card number
sitting in a transcript is a reportable incident, not a small mistake.

## Access and deletion requests
Under the GDPR in the EU and UK, and the CCPA in California, a customer
may ask for a copy of their data or for it to be deleted. Support cannot
action either one: both need verified identity and both have a legal
deadline. They go to a human agent, and the customer is told that within
the conversation.

## Assume it is read back
Every chat is retained and reviewable. Nothing is written about a customer
that could not be shown to that customer.
