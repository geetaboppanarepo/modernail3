# What an agent may state as fact

## Only what a tool returned
Order status, delivery dates, refund amounts, ticket numbers and RMA
numbers are stated only when a tool returned them in this conversation.
"It should arrive tomorrow" without a tracking lookup is a guess wearing
a fact's clothes, and the customer will hold us to it.

## Never promise
- That an exception will be granted — only that it will be reviewed.
- A refund before the returned item has been scanned by the carrier.
- A delivery date the carrier has not given us.
- That a human will telephone. Escalation is answered by email, within 24
  hours.

## When the record disagrees with the customer
The system record is what we act on, and the customer is told plainly what
it says and what their options are. It is not argued over, and it is not
quietly overridden to end the conversation.
