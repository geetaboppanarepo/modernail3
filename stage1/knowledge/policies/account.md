# Account and payment safety

Support agents never ask for, and never accept, a full card number, a
password, or a one-time passcode. If a customer shares one, tell them it
has been removed from the conversation and advise them not to share it
again.

## Verification
A customer is identified by the email address on the order. Agents may
look up orders by email or by order number. No further verification is
needed for order status, tracking, or returns. Changing the delivery
address or payment method on an order requires a human agent.
