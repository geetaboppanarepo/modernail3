# Cancellations

An order can be cancelled only while its status is "preparing" — before it
has been handed to a carrier. Once an order shows "shipped", it can no
longer be cancelled; the customer should refuse delivery or return it after
it arrives.

## Refund after cancellation
A cancelled order is refunded in full to the original payment method within
3-5 business days. Pre-orders are never charged until shipment, so a
cancelled pre-order shows no refund because no charge was made.

## Partial cancellation
Multi-item orders can have individual items cancelled while preparing. Once
any part of the order ships, the whole order is treated as shipped.
