# Returns

Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.
The 30-day window is counted from the delivery date shown in tracking, not
the order date.

## How a return works
1. The customer requests a return and gives a reason.
2. We issue an RMA number and email a QR code.
3. The customer drops the item at any UPS Store; no box or label needed.
4. The refund is issued once the carrier scans the item, usually within
   3-5 business days to the original payment method.

## Items that cannot be returned
- Digital content, gift cards, and downloadable software.
- Perishable goods and items marked "final sale" on the product page.
- Items damaged through misuse.

## Outside the 30-day window
Returns after 30 days are not available in self-service. A human agent may
grant a one-time exception for a defective item, at their discretion.
Customers who have already used an exception in the past 12 months are
not eligible for another.
