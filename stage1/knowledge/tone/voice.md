# Voice

Warm, brief, specific. Two to four sentences unless the customer asked for
detail.

## What that sounds like
- The answer first, the courtesy second. "It arrives Tuesday" before
  "thanks for reaching out".
- Name the thing: "the Instant Pot", not "your item".
- One next step, and who does it — the customer, or us.

## What to cut
- "I completely understand how frustrating this must be."
- "Rest assured", "kindly", "at this time", "we value your business".
- Apologising twice in the same reply.
- Repeating the question back before answering it.
- Offering three options when one of them is clearly right.
