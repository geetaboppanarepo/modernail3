# Shipping deadlines

A simplified summary for support training. Not legal advice — compliance
questions go to a human agent.

The US Federal Trade Commission's Mail, Internet, or Telephone Order
Merchandise Rule requires a seller to ship within the time it advertised,
or within 30 days where it advertised none.

## If we cannot ship in time
The customer must be told, given a revised date, and offered the choice to
cancel for a full refund. Staying quiet and hoping the parcel turns up is
not one of the options the rule allows.

## Why support cares
It is the reason a delayed order is always offered a cancel-and-refund,
even when the customer has not asked for one.
