# Gift cards

A simplified summary for support training. Not legal advice — compliance
questions go to a human agent.

Under the US Credit CARD Act of 2009, gift card funds may not expire for
at least five years from the date the money was loaded, and dormancy fees
are tightly limited.

## What that means here
- A gift card purchase is not refundable, and the balance on it does not
  expire. Never tell a customer their balance has lapsed — balances are
  looked up, not assumed.
- A balance is not converted to cash, except where a state law requires it
  below a small threshold. That is a human agent's decision, not ours.
- A gift card bought with a stolen card may be frozen. That is a fraud
  matter and is escalated, not explained.
